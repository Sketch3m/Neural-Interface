# Topic map

## Neural interface / plasticity thread

The source discussion proposes a multi-stage research architecture: read or estimate a learner's current state; model recipient-specific representational geometry; examine alternative neural organizations; use structured representations such as Bridge-HRR; guide reconstruction through a state-dependent channel; and check consolidation after removing the external teacher. The proposed stack is conceptual. Its numerical results came from a synthetic dynamical-brain simulation, not human experiments.

## Connections to encoding literature

The related language conversation distinguishes decoding intended speech from writing semantic or lexical content into a brain. It discusses distributed semantic, lexical, phonological, and articulatory representations and notes that those representations can interact. The conversation references studies, but this repository index does not independently verify their claims or citations.

## Computational representation work

The HDC/VSA conversations explore compositional representations, nested programs, geometric mappings, resonance, and continual learning. They may provide computational analogies for a neural-interface research program, but successful AI compression or toy simulations do not establish biological transfer or safe brain stimulation.

## Suggested research questions

1. What measurable neural target would count as a successful write operation: a percept, a learned decoder, a stable representation, or later independent recall?
2. How can experiments distinguish externally cued performance from durable plasticity and generalization?
3. Which representational properties should transfer across learners, and which require recipient-specific calibration?
4. How should uncertainty, adverse effects, and failure cases be measured before any stimulation claim is made?
5. Which claims in the conversations are supported by peer-reviewed evidence, which are engineering hypotheses, and which are only synthetic demonstrations?
